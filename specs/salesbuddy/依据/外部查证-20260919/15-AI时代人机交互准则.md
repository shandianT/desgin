## 1. Microsoft：Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction（18 条）与 HAX Toolkit

### 1.1 来源
- 论文：Saleema Amershi, Dan Weld, Mihaela Vorvoreanu, Adam Fourney, Besmira Nushi, Penny Collisson, Jina Suh, Shamsi Iqbal, Paul N. Bennett, Kori Inkpen, Jaime Teevan, Ruth Kikin-Gil, Eric Horvitz. "Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction." CHI 2019（获 Honorable Mention）。
  - 读到的 PDF：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf （由 …/uploads/prod/2019/01/… 301 跳转而来）
  - 出版物页：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/
  - ACM DL（doi 10.1145/3290605.3300233）被拦截，未读。
- HAX Design Library（18 条准则逐条页面 + 设计模式 + 案例）：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/library/
- HAX Toolkit 首页：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/ ；Design Patterns：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/design-patterns/

论文摘要原文（节选）："We propose 18 generally applicable design guidelines for human-AI interaction. These guidelines are validated through multiple rounds of evaluation including a user study with 49 design practitioners who tested the guidelines against 20 popular AI-infused products."
（译：我们提出 18 条普适的人机（人—AI）交互设计准则，经多轮评估验证，包括 49 位设计从业者对 20 款流行 AI 产品的测试。）

论文对四个阶段的定义原文："We organized the guidelines into four top-level categories based on when during the user's interaction they applied: 'Initially' (Guideline 1 & Guideline 2), 'During interaction' (Guideline 3 - Guideline 6), 'When wrong' (Guideline 7 - Guideline 11), and 'Over time' (Guideline 12 - Guideline 18)."
（译：按准则在用户交互过程中的适用时点分为四大类：「初始阶段」G1–G2、「交互过程中」G3–G6、「出错时」G7–G11、「随时间推移」G12–G18。）

### 1.2 18 条准则原文（HAX Design Library 与论文表 1 一致）与中译

**Initially（初始阶段）**
- G1 "Make clear what the system can do." — "Help the user understand what the AI system is capable of doing."
  译：说清系统能做什么——帮助用户理解 AI 系统具备哪些能力。
- G2 "Make clear how well the system can do what it can do." — "Help the user understand how often the AI system may make mistakes."
  译：说清系统能做到什么程度——帮助用户理解 AI 系统可能多常出错。

**During interaction（交互过程中）**
- G3 "Time services based on context." — "Time when to act or interrupt based on the user's current task and environment."
  译：依据情境把握服务时机——根据用户当前任务与环境决定何时行动或打断。
- G4 "Show contextually relevant information." — "Display information relevant to the user's current task and environment."
  译：展示与情境相关的信息。
- G5 "Match relevant social norms." — "Ensure the experience is delivered in a way that users would expect, given their social and cultural context."
  译：符合相应的社会规范——以用户在其社会文化语境下所期待的方式提供体验。
- G6 "Mitigate social biases." — "Ensure the AI system's language and behaviors do not reinforce undesirable and unfair stereotypes and biases."
  译：减少社会偏见——确保 AI 的语言与行为不强化不当、不公的刻板印象与偏见。

**When wrong（出错时）**
- G7 "Support efficient invocation." — "Make it easy to invoke or request the AI system's services when needed."
  译：便于唤起——需要时能轻松调用或请求 AI 服务。
- G8 "Support efficient dismissal." — "Make it easy to dismiss or ignore undesired AI system services."
  译：便于忽略——不想要的 AI 服务可轻松关闭或忽略。
- G9 "Support efficient correction." — "Make it easy to edit, refine, or recover when the AI system is wrong."
  译：便于纠正——AI 出错时可轻松编辑、细化或恢复。
- G10 "Scope services when in doubt." — "Engage in disambiguation or gracefully degrade the AI system's services when uncertain about a user's goals."
  译：不确定时收缩服务范围——对用户目标不确定时进行消歧或优雅降级。
- G11 "Make clear why the system did what it did." — "Enable the user to access an explanation of why the AI system behaved as it did."
  译：说清系统为何这样做——让用户能获得 AI 行为原因的解释。

**Over time（随时间推移）**
- G12 "Remember recent interactions." — "Maintain short term memory and allow the user to make efficient references to that memory."
  译：记住近期交互——保持短期记忆并让用户能高效引用它。
- G13 "Learn from user behavior." — "Personalize the user's experience by learning from their actions over time."
  译：从用户行为中学习——随时间从用户行为中学习以个性化体验。
- G14 "Update and adapt cautiously." — "Limit disruptive changes when updating and adapting the AI system's behaviors."
  译：谨慎更新与调整——更新、调整 AI 行为时限制破坏性变化。
- G15 "Encourage granular feedback." — "Enable the user to provide feedback indicating their preferences during regular interaction with the AI system."
  译：鼓励细粒度反馈——让用户在日常交互中就能表达偏好。
- G16 "Convey the consequences of user actions." — "Immediately update or convey how user actions will impact future behaviors of the AI system."
  译：传达用户操作的后果——即时更新或告知用户操作将如何影响 AI 未来行为。
- G17 "Provide global controls." — "Allow the user to globally customize what the AI system monitors and how it behaves."
  译：提供全局控制——允许用户整体设定 AI 监测什么、如何行为。
- G18 "Notify users about changes." — "Inform the user when the AI system adds or updates its capabilities."
  译：告知变更——AI 新增或更新能力时通知用户。

论文表 1 中与企业产品相关的两条示例原文：
- G9：[Voice Assistants] "Once my request for a reminder was processed I saw the ability to edit my reminder in the UI that was displayed. Small text underneath stated 'Tap to Edit'…"（提醒被处理后，界面上立刻给出「点按编辑」。）
- G10：[Autocomplete] "It usually provides 3-4 suggestions instead of directly auto completing it for you"（给 3～4 个候选，而不是直接替你补全。）

### 1.3 HAX Design Library 的设计模式（读到的两页）
- G9「Support efficient correction」页：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/support-efficient-correction/ 。页面给出的示例："for an AI-powered navigation app that can recommend fast routes to a user's destination, make it easy for the user to manually edit a recommended route or part of the route"。列出的模式（名称抄自页面）：G9-A "Switch classification decisions"（切换分类判定）、G9-B "Rich and detailed edits"（丰富细致的编辑）、G9-C "Undo automated actions"（撤销自动化动作）、G9-E "Batch-editing data"（批量编辑数据）。（页面另有一条 G9-D，抓取工具返回的名称疑似解析错误，此处不抄录。）
- G11「Make clear why the system did what it did」页：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/haxtoolkit/guideline/make-clear-why-the-system-did-what-it-did/ 。页面提醒：解释可能"increase user trust excessively"，造成"over-reliance on the system and over-inflated expectations"（即自动化偏见）；解释可以是"global, explaining the entire system, or local, explaining each output"。七个模式：G11-A "Local explanations"、G11-B "Global explanations"、G11-C "Present properties of system outputs"、G11-D "Map input attributes to system outputs"、G11-E "Map user behaviors to system outputs"、G11-F "Example-based explanations"、G11-G "'What if?' explanations"。
- Design Patterns 页说明："Patterns are currently available for eight Guidelines"，"The pattern number inherits the Guideline number, so you know that all patterns starting with G1 help you implement Guideline 1."
- HAX Toolkit 四件套自述：Guidelines——"Best practices for how AI systems should behave during interaction. Use them to guide your AI product planning."；Design Library——"Learn the Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction and how to apply them, using patterns and examples."；Workbook——"Work together with your team to prioritize which Guidelines to implement in your product."；Playbook——"For applications using natural language processing, identify common failures so you can plan for mitigating them."
- Design Library 的筛选维度（页面原文）：按内容类型 Guidelines／Design Patterns／Examples；按产品类别；按 AI 类型（Classification、Generative AI、Large Language Model 等）；按目标（Appropriate reliance、Fairness、Personalization、Reliability、Transparency）。「Appropriate reliance（恰当依赖）」是微软近年的核心提法。

### 1.4 Microsoft Responsible AI 六原则（原文）
来源：https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/ai/principles-and-approach
- Fairness: "AI systems should treat all people fairly."（公平：AI 应公平对待所有人）
- Reliability and safety: "AI systems should perform reliably and safely."（可靠与安全）
- Privacy and security: "AI systems should be secure and respect privacy."（隐私与安全）
- Inclusiveness: "AI systems should empower everyone and engage all people, regardless of their backgrounds."（包容）
- Transparency: "AI systems should be understandable."（透明：AI 应当可被理解）
- Accountability: "People should be accountable for AI systems."（问责：人要对 AI 系统负责）
页面同时说明透明是为了让人"correctly understand the capabilities of a system"，问责是为了"oversight so that humans can be accountable and in control"。

### 1.7 微软最独特的一两点
1. 把准则按「时间轴」组织（初始／过程中／出错时／长期），并把「出错时」当成一等公民（G7–G11 五条），承认 AI 必然出错，设计重心在「唤起—忽略—纠正—收缩—解释」这一组配套动作。
2. 「Appropriate reliance（恰当依赖）」：解释不是越多越好，G11 页面明确警告解释会导致过度信任与自动化偏见。

### 1.8 对「AI 出草稿 → 人确认 → 归档」最有用的三条
- G9 便于纠正 + G9-C 撤销自动化动作：拜访草稿的每个字段可就地编辑，归档后可回滚。
- G10 不确定时收缩：听不清就给候选值让销售选，不硬猜；两次不成就降级为手工表单。
- G2／G11 说清做得多好、为什么：象限、风险、画像结果旁必须有「依据」入口，并用分档而非百分比表述把握程度；G16 让销售知道自己的确认与修改会怎样影响后续 Agent 行为。

### 2.2 章节结构（二手：搜索摘要，措辞未核对）
六章："User Needs + Defining Success"（识别用户需求、寻找 AI 机会、设计奖励函数）、"Data Collection + Evaluation"、"Mental Models"（介绍 AI 系统并为系统随时间变化设定预期）、"Explainability + Trust"（解释 AI 系统；决定是否、何时、如何展示模型置信度）、"Feedback + Control"（设计反馈与控制机制以改进 AI 与体验）、"Errors + Graceful Failure"（识别并诊断 AI 错误与情境错误，传达前进的路）。Patterns 页按"how to get started with human-centered AI / when and how to use AI in products / how to onboard users to new AI features / how to explain AI systems to users"组织。搜索摘要提及的模式名："Set the right expectations"、"Explain for trust"、"Determine how to show model confidence"、"Give users control"、"Let users give feedback"——均未逐字核实。

### 2.3 已读原文：「Interaction Design Policies」工作表（User Needs + Defining Success 章配套）
这是 Guidebook 生成式 AI 更新中最可操作的一份材料，全文可读，摘录如下（英文原文，后附中译）：
- 目的："Interaction design policies are the set of criteria that govern the user's experience at the point in a critical user journey (CUJ) where the user interacts with an AI system in a product."（交互设计策略＝在关键用户旅程中用户与 AI 交互那一刻，约束体验的一组准则。）
- 三步："1. Identify critical moments — Review and prioritize situations or events in your product or critical user journeys (CUJs) where your users interact with your AI system."；"2. Align on your interaction design policies"；"3. Define UX and ML requirements — Translate the interaction design policies into actionable interventions or interactions in the user experience and requirements for the AI systems in your product."
- 关键时刻的优先级："consider prioritizing critical moments that can change the course of the user journey, or where AI outcomes can be more uncertain."（优先处理会改变旅程走向、或 AI 结果更不确定的时刻。）
- 一条策略由四部分构成（原文）：
  - "Acceptable Actions — Given an input, what are the types of actions, use cases, or tasks that the AI system should be capable of performing?"（可接受的动作）
  - "Unacceptable Actions — Given a user input, what are the types of actions, use cases, or tasks that the AI system should not perform? These are the range of tasks that a user might unintentionally ask the model to perform."（不可接受的动作，包括用户无意中会要求的）
  - "Thresholds Of Uncertainty — What are the thresholds of uncertainty or confidence, above which a prediction can be surfaced? These are the weak predictions that can reduce performance or slow down a user, but not actively harm them."（不确定性阈值：高于多少才把预测呈现出来）
  - "Vulnerabilities — What are the different types of errors that the model can produce? What kinds of risks are users unwittingly vulnerable to? These are outputs that cause the system to fail, or outcomes that need to be mitigated altogether."（脆弱点：哪些错误必须整体消除）
- 模板句式（原文）："We want people to successfully use GenAI for/to…"；"We don't want people to intentionally or unintentionally use GenAI for/to…"；"When GenAI predictions are weak, people won't mind being asked to…"；"An incorrect or wrong GenAI prediction can harm individuals and groups when / if / by…"
- 参与者："Stakeholders to invite: UX Designers, UX Researchers, Product Managers, Software Engineers, and ML Developers. When possible, include perspectives from Privacy, Security, Legal, Public Policy, Sales & Marketing, Quality Assurance, and Trust & Safety…"

- Gemini 应用「Our approach」（https://gemini.google/our-approach/）、Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform「Design agents in Agent Studio」（https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/agent-studio/design-agents）、Google AI Principles（https://ai.google/principles/）均被拦截，未读。

### 2.5 Google 最独特的一两点
1. 「交互设计策略」把 AI 体验定义在「关键时刻」上，并用「可接受／不可接受动作、不确定性阈值、脆弱点」四格把设计、模型与法务要求绑定在同一张表里——这是把「AI 该做什么、不该做什么、多不确定就不要出手」写成可评审文档的方法。
2. 六章结构以「心智模型—可解释与信任—反馈与控制—错误与优雅失败」为主干（章节名为二手），与微软 18 条的四阶段互为补充。

### 2.6 对「AI 出草稿 → 人确认 → 归档」最有用的三条
- 为「拜访口述→结构化」「象限重算」「任务下发」三个关键时刻各写一张交互设计策略表（可接受／不可接受／不确定性阈值／脆弱点）。
- 「Thresholds Of Uncertainty」句式直接可用："当抽取把握不足时，销售不介意被要求……（从候选中选一个／补一个词）"，作为字段级置信阈值的产品定义。
- 「Unacceptable Actions」明确列入：不得代销售向客户发送任何内容、不得改分改象限、不得跨权限读取客户。

### 3.1 来源

- 页面：https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/machine-learning （2023-05-02 合并为单页，2026-06-08 小幅更新）
- 实际读取载体：上述页面的 JSON 数据端点 https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/generative-ai.json 与 …/machine-learning.json（HIG 网站正文即由该数据渲染，内容一致）。

### 3.2 Generative AI 页原文（加粗句为 Apple 的准则句，后附中译）
Best practices
- "**Design your experience responsibly.** … Orient your design process around crafting AI experiences that are inclusive, designed with care, and protect privacy."（负责任地设计：围绕包容、审慎、保护隐私来组织设计过程。）
- "**Keep people in control.** While AI can manipulate and create content, respect people's agency and ensure they remain in charge of decision making and the overall experience. Honor their requests when in scope and the expected output is clear, and handle sensitive content carefully. Give them the ability to dismiss new content they don't want, and revert or retry content transformations or other actions they don't agree with. Clearly identify when and where you use AI."（让人掌控：尊重人的能动性，确保人始终主导决策与整体体验；让人能关掉不想要的内容、撤销或重试不认可的转换与动作；清楚标明何时何处使用了 AI。）
- "**Design engaging and useful generative features.** … Offer generative features when and where they provide clear and specific value, like time savings, improved communication, or enhanced creativity."（只在有明确具体价值处提供生成功能。）
- "**Ensure a great experience even when generative features aren't available or people opt not to use them.** … When possible, consider offering a non-AI fallback."（AI 不可用或用户不用时也要好用；尽量提供非 AI 的回退路径。）

Transparency
- "**Communicate where your app uses AI.** Letting people know when and where your app uses AI sets expectations and gives people the opportunity to knowingly choose to use an AI-powered feature. Never trick someone into thinking they're interacting with or viewing content authored by a human if they're actually interacting with AI. Ensure your approach to disclosure aligns with any regulations in the regions where you offer your app."（告知何处用了 AI；绝不让人误以为在与人交互或在看人写的内容；披露方式要符合各地区法规。）
- "**Set clear expectations about what your AI-powered feature can and can't do.** … If your feature has known limitations, let people know up front, show them how to get good results, and explain why inferior results occur."（说清能做与不能做；已知局限提前告知，示范如何得到好结果，解释为何结果差。）

Privacy
- "**Choose a model type that fits your feature's needs and protects people's privacy.** … process as much information locally as possible, and minimize what's shared. Be transparent by making sure people know their information may be sent to a server, showing them what's shared, and helping them understand what data may be stored off-device or used for training."（尽量本地处理、最小化共享；让人知道哪些数据会上传、存储、用于训练。）
- "**Ask permission before using personal information and usage data.** … After obtaining permission, use the minimum data you need and always offer a clear way to opt out of its use."（先征得许可，用最少的数据，始终可退出。）
- "**Clearly disclose how your app and its model use and store personal information.**"

Inputs
- "**Guide people on how to use your generative feature.** … offer diverse, predefined example inputs that hint at what's possible for a feature."（用示例输入引导。）
- "**Raise awareness about and minimize the chance of hallucinations.** … it's important to clearly communicate that AI-generated content may contain errors. … Avoid using AI-generated content in situations where a possible hallucination could misinform and harm someone."（明示 AI 内容可能有误；限定生成范围以降低幻觉；高风险处不用生成内容。）
- "**Consider consequences and get permission before performing irreversible or potentially problematic tasks.** Before performing a task, consider whether a mistake or the inability to reverse the action might cause more work or stress for people. Avoid automating destructive actions, like deleting photos, and actions that are hard to undo, like making a purchase on a person's behalf. Generally, ask for confirmation before performing a significant action on someone's behalf."（不可逆或有问题的任务先取得许可；不要自动化破坏性、难撤销的动作；代人执行重大动作前一般要确认。）

Outputs
- "**Make it easy for people to refine or revert generated results, and acknowledge when their corrections take effect.** For example, surfacing controls like Edit, Undo, Retry, or Adjust near generated content preserves people's agency while still letting them benefit from automation. When people adjust or personalize output, provide a clear signal that their action had an effect."（在生成内容旁给 编辑／撤销／重试／调整；修正生效要有明确信号。）
- "**Help people improve requests when blocked or undesirable results occur.**"
- "**Reduce unexpected and harmful outcomes with thoughtful design and thorough testing.**"
- "**Strive to avoid replicating copyrighted content.**"
- "**Factor processing time into your design.** … Generative models typically take longer to produce a result, so design a loading experience or generate in the background while a person uses another part of the app."（把延迟设计进去：加载体验或后台生成。）
- "**Consider giving specific, reassuring feedback during generation.** … instead of 'Processing…', say 'Finding substitutions for ingredients' or 'Summarizing key themes from your notes.' … If something goes wrong, describe what happened in plain language and offer a clear next step."（生成中用具体状态而非「处理中」；出错用白话说明并给下一步。）
- "**Consider offering alternate versions of results.** … Offering people a choice can give them a greater sense of control and help bridge the gap between the model's interpretation and what someone actually wants."（提供多个版本供选择。）

Continuous improvement
- "**Let people share feedback on outputs.** … Always make providing feedback voluntary. Respect people's time by placing a feedback affordance in a clear location that doesn't interrupt the experience. Consider offering a quick and easy way to give positive and negative feedback, like simple thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons."（反馈自愿、不打断；点赞点踩。）
- "**Design flexible, adaptable features.** … separate your model from your user experience so you can swap out the model for other models over time."（模型与体验解耦。）

### 3.3 Machine learning 页中与本项目直接相关的原文
- 角色判断（Critical or complementary／Private or public／Proactive or reactive／Visible or invisible／Dynamic or static）："In general, the more central a feature is to your app's purpose … the more people expect accurate and reliable results."；"Because people don't ask for the results that a proactive feature provides, they may have less tolerance for low-quality information."
- Explicit feedback："**Request explicit feedback only when necessary.**"；"**Always make providing explicit feedback a voluntary task.**"；"**Use simple, direct language to describe each explicit feedback option and its consequences.** Avoid using imprecise terms such as dislike"；"**Act immediately when you receive explicit feedback and persist the resulting changes.**"
- Mistakes："It's inevitable that your app will make mistakes."；"**Understand the significance of a mistake's consequences.** … Show empathy by providing corrective actions or tools that match the seriousness of the mistake."；"**Be especially careful to avoid mistakes in proactive features.**"
- Corrections："**Give people familiar, easy ways to make corrections.**"；"**Provide immediate value when people make a correction.** … be sure to persist the updates so people don't have to make the same corrections again."；"**Never rely on corrections to make up for low-quality results.**"；"**When possible, use guided corrections instead of freeform corrections.** Guided corrections suggest specific alternatives, so they require less user effort … An example of guided correction is a speech-to-text feature that gives people a list of alternative text completions from which to choose."（语音转文字给候选项即「引导式纠正」。）
- Multiple options："**Prefer diverse options.**"；"**In general, avoid providing too many options.**"；"**List the most likely option first.**"
- Confidence："If you're not sure how your confidence values correlate with the quality of your results, it's not a good idea to convey confidence to people."；"**In general, translate confidence values into concepts that people already understand.** … displaying '97% match' next to the new song … doesn't communicate enough information … providing an attribution that clearly identifies the behavior — such as 'Because you listen to pop music' — can be more actionable."；"If you must display confidence directly, consider expressing it in terms of semantic categories … like 'high chance' and 'low chance'"；"**Consider changing how you present results based on different confidence thresholds.** … when confidence is lower, the feature asks people to confirm whether the photos contain the person before showing more."；"**When you know that confidence values correspond to result quality, you generally want to avoid showing results when confidence is low.**"
- Attribution："An attribution expresses the underlying basis or rationale for a result, without explaining exactly how a model works."；"**Keep attributions factual and based on objective analysis.** … use an attribution like 'Because you've read nonfiction' instead of … 'Because you love nonfiction.'"；"**In general, avoid technical or statistical jargon.**"
- Limitations："**Help people establish realistic expectations.**"；"**Demonstrate how to get the best results.**"；"**Explain how limitations can cause unsatisfactory results.**"；"**Consider telling people when limitations are resolved.**"

### 3.4 Apple 最独特的一两点
1. 「置信度要翻译成人能理解的概念」：明确反对裸露百分比，主张用「因为你……」式的归因（attribution）或「高／低可能」语义分档，并按置信阈值切换呈现方式（高置信直接展示，低置信先让人确认）。
2. 「引导式纠正优先于自由纠正」，并把语音转写给候选项作为标准范例；同时强调不可逆动作前要许可、修正要即时生效并持久化。

### 3.5 对「AI 出草稿 → 人确认 → 归档」最有用的三条
- 「Keep people in control」＋「get permission before performing irreversible…」＋「Edit, Undo, Retry, or Adjust near generated content」：草稿卡片旁固定放编辑／撤销／重试；写表、发任务、外发消息前确认。
- 「Consider changing how you present results based on different confidence thresholds」：字段抽取置信高→预填并标记；置信低→留空或给候选，要求销售选择。
- 「Consider giving specific, reassuring feedback during generation」：转写与结构化过程用「正在提取拜访要点 12／16」等具体状态。

### 4.2 原文与中译
定位："Carbon for AI is an extension of the Carbon system, designed to give AI instances in IBM products a visually and behaviorally distinct identity."（给产品中的 AI 实例一个在视觉与行为上都有区别的身份。）"Carbon for AI is the framework for identifying AI-generated content and delivering explainability in our products."

四条总则（标题即准则）：
- "**Use the AI label and styling to mark any instances of AI usage** — Transparency of AI presence is key to user trust. Having a consistent identity helps build awareness and anticipation of AI presence across our experiences."（用 AI 标签和样式标记任何 AI 使用实例；AI 在场的透明是信任的关键。）
- "**Provide transparency of AI at each level of the experience** — Whether AI exists within a single word or across the entire page, users should clearly understand how pervasive an AI is implemented."（在体验的每一层级提供透明：无论 AI 只影响一个词还是整页，用户都应清楚 AI 渗透到什么程度。）
- "**Integrate explainability into the overall user workflow and experience** — This integration requires you to minimize distractions and show explanations only when needed or requested by the user."（把可解释性融入工作流：尽量少打扰，只在需要或用户请求时展示解释。）
- "**Don't use the Carbon for AI styling as decoration** — While these new styling elements are enticing, they are strictly intended to identify any instances of AI being used in an experience."（AI 样式不是装饰，只用于标识 AI。）

样式隐喻："Carbon for AI styling builds on the core Carbon library principles, using light as a metaphor to 'illuminate' AI-generated content."（用「光」来照亮 AI 生成内容：亮度、辉光、渐变。）配套"a new suite of color and style tokens"（AI 专用色彩与样式 token，位于主主题内）。

可解释性："Explainability is a set of processes and methods that make AI decisions more comprehensible to humans."；"**Use the AI label as the path to explainability** — The AI label is the primary indicator to communicate that AI is present, and it's how users can access explainability content."；"**Start with summarized explanations** — As a first layer of explainability, use the explainability pop-over attached to the AI label to provide a quick, in-context option to explain AI results for a particular instance."（以摘要解释起步，AI 标签即通往解释的入口。）

AI label 组件（原名 AI slug）："The AI label is a component used as an indicator of AI instances in the UI. It is intended for any scenario where AI is generating content to reinforce AI transparency, accountability, and explainability at any interface level."
- When to use：Mark AI-generated content／Provides a consistent visual reference point for AI／A pathway to explainability。
- When not to use："Don't use the AI label as decoration"；"Don't modify AI label behaviors"；"**Don't use the AI label as a trigger for AI actions** — The AI label is not a UI icon and should not be used with buttons or other call-to-actions to trigger an AI action like 'regenerate.'"（AI 标签不是动作按钮。）
- 可见性层级：Focused（"Use for individual occurrences of AI within a section"）与 Broad（"Use for summarization of AI usage across a section of a page."）；两者并用的条件："Individual AI instances still need to be explained. The user needs to distinguish between AI and non-AI content. The user needs to act on AI at an instance level."
- 放置规则：容器右上；输入框右侧居中；与其它图标并列时置于最左；数据表按整表／整列／整行／单元格四级放置；表单按整表或嵌入到 AI 生成的字段。
- 本地化："The abbreviation AI in the AI label … can be localized … AI in Chinese, Indonesia, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Spanish LA; IA in European Spanish…; KI in German; ИИ in Russian, Bulgarian"。
- 解释弹层内容模板四段："Overview / Supporting details / Artifacts and resources / Additional actions"（概览／支撑细节／工件与资源／更多动作）。
- 状态："The AI label should never be disabled as part of a disabled or read-only state."

Revert（恢复 AI 建议）："These components can toggle between the AI variant and the default variant depending on the user's interaction. If the user manually overrides the AI-suggested content then the component will change from the AI variant to the default variant. Once edited, the user should still be able to switch back to the initially AI-generated content via a revert to AI button."；"Users can manually override AI-suggested content, but they should always be able to programmatically switch back to the initially suggested AI content. In these instances, when the content is overridden by the user, the AI presence styling is removed and the AI label is replaced by a 'revert' action icon-only button."（人工改写后 AI 样式与标签消失、出现「恢复为 AI 建议」按钮；点击可恢复 AI 内容及其标签。）

AI 组件要求："Each AI component is required to have an embedded AI label and explainability popover that alerts users to AI-generated content. When the AI label is turned on, the component transforms to the AI visual style but still functions like the standard one."（有 AI 变体的核心组件：Checkbox、Form、Select、Data table、Modal、Tag、Date picker、Number input、Text input、Dropdown、Radio button、Tile。）

无障碍："When designing an AI experience, ask if the user knows AI is being used. The base principle of Carbon for AI is to mark where AI is present while providing explainability whenever available."；"Light spread is limited for readability"。

AI chat："AI chat is a conversational framework between a user and an AI that can aid in creating tasks, finding insights, tracking documents, and more."（预览状态，代码在 carbon-design-system/carbon-ai-chat。）

### 4.3 IBM 最独特的一两点
1. 把「AI 在场」做成设计系统级的基础设施：专用 token、光的隐喻、AI 标签组件、每个表单类组件都有 AI 变体；标签同时是解释入口，且明确禁止用作装饰或动作触发器。

- 解释弹层四段模板：概览（这条象限判定是什么）、支撑细节（依据的拜访事实）、工件与资源（原始语音转写、拜访记录链接）、更多动作（申诉／反馈）。

## 5. Ant Design X（RICH 设计范式）、Semi Design、TDesign

### 5.1 Ant Design X：来源
- 官网 https://x.ant.design/docs/spec/introduce/ 等被拦截；github.com 网页与 API 被拦截，但 git clone https://github.com/ant-design/x.git 成功。
- 读到的原文位于仓库 packages/x/docs/spec/ 目录（24 篇中文 + 对应英文）：introduce、role-design、authentic-consistent、smooth-natural、human-touch、intention-introduce、define-intention-type、provide-intention-expectation、guide-intention-expression、conversation-design、start、follow-up、hint、confirm、error、end、hybrid-ui-design、wake-up-ai-icon、wake-up-welcome-message、expression-user-input、expression-user-send、confirm-generation-process、feedback-result-display、feedback-result-application。README：https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ant-design/x/main/README.md

### 5.2 RICH 定义（原文，中文为原生）
- "RICH 是我们提出来的一个 AI 界面设计范式，好比 WIMP 范式之于图形用户界面。"
- 四要素："**Intention 意图：** AI 能够听懂并理解用户的意图，协助用户自动完成方案计划和步骤拆解，进而推动执行"；"**Role 角色：** AI 扮演了某种身份角色，来匹配用户的意图，也保障与用户的互动是顺畅、符合预期的"；"**Conversation 会话：** 用户的模糊意图通过会话来逐步与 AI 对焦、拆解，用户的指令也结合其中"；"**Hybrid UI 混合界面：** 用户的执行动作和机器的结果输出与反馈承载在融合了多种交互方式的界面当中"。
- 立论："人机交互的本质是用户通过执行某种动作或行为输入给机器，机器理解并完成诉求后产出结果给用户，用户评估是否符合要求，如果符合，一个交互单元就完成了闭环。"；"AI 时代，体验还取决于机器是否听得懂我的意思，是否讲话比较好听等等一系列隐形的体验。"

### 5.3 Intention 意图设计（原文要点）
- "并非所有意图都适合会话式交互，有时简单点击在某些场景下比多轮对话更为高效。"
- 意图分类："从用户意图清晰度，可分为意图清晰与意图模糊"；"从用户与系统间交互目的，可分为咨询信息类与执行任务类"。
- 介入方式："**Do 适合内嵌式：** 以界面操作为主，偶尔唤起AI快捷指令，更适合意图上清晰与行为上做管为主的。"；"**Chat 适合独立式：** 以自然语言为主，几乎没有界面操作。更适合意图上模糊与行为上查看搜索为主的。"；"**Chat+Do 适合助手式：** 自然语言和界面操作均衡配合使用。"
- 提供意图预期："对用户的意图进行有效引导显得尤为重要，旨在帮助用户明确AI的能力边界，从而建立符合 AI 可实现范围的意图预期。"
- 槽位匹配策略："**意图与槽位精准匹配：** 若用户意图的可靠度高且所有必填槽位均已成功填写，系统将直接发送指令到下游服务，执行用户请求。"；"**意图匹配到多个类似槽位：** 当用户意图或关键槽位的可靠度较低时，系统将回复意图或槽位确认信息，以请求用户进一步澄清或提供额外信息"；"**意图未匹配到槽位：** …采用对话转移或回复兜底话术策略"。

### 5.4 Role 角色设计（原文要点）
- "角色塑造的精髓在于一致性、自然性与情感化。"
- 真实一致："角色的行为模式和语言风格都应该符合我们对角色的基础背景设定。在与用户的互动中，不同的对话环境下，保持角色前后的一致性，避免用户产生跳出感"。四个一致：性格特征一致、视觉表达一致、语言风格一致、行为模式一致。
- 流畅自然：语法自然、语气自然、逻辑自然。
- 有人情味：情感的适应性、语言的丰富度、互动的个性化、视觉的表现力（适用场景以陪伴、客服、教育为主）。

### 5.5 Conversation 会话设计（原文要点）
三条通用原则：
- "**1.信息充分且真实**"："AI 需提供真实的信息——对话交互系统必须基于事实和数据提供信息，以确保用户能够依据真实、准确的信息做出决策。这要求 AI 在处理用户请求时，必须写明引用的数据源，并确保信息的时效性和准确性。"；"AI 需告知自己能力界限——AI 应明确告知用户其功能和限制，避免用户对 AI 能力产生误解。这包括在 AI 无法提供确切答案或执行特定任务时，诚实地向用户说明情况，并提供备选方案或建议。"
- "**2.话术要清晰易懂**"：任务相关性、词汇的普及性、术语的一致性、句式的简洁性、清晰度。
- "**3.自然友好并且尊重用户**"：自然交流、尊重与认可、敏感话题回避、"审慎处理内容——对于用户未主动请求的信息或内容，应保持谨慎态度，避免过度干预或提供不适当的信息。"

开始："首要任务是迅速设定正确的期望，引导用户发现 AI 的功能，并将对话的主动权交还给用户。"；三目标：热情迎接用户、明确设定预期（"可以简单列举 2-3 个高频功能，但避免详述细节"）、赋予用户控制权。

追问："若用户所提供的信息不足以支撑 AI 完成既定的任务目标，AI 必须采取主动措施，通过精心设计的询问来引导用户提供额外的槽位信息。"二次确认的适用场景："当误解用户意图可能导致严重后果时（例如，涉及姓名、地址或用户授权分享的文本）；在执行不可逆操作之前（例如，删除用户数据或完成交易）。"

提示："应避免教导用户如何与 AI 进行交流。理想的交互方式是让 AI 适应用户的自然语言，而不是要求用户学习并使用特定的命令或短语。"；"给提示而不是命令"。

确认（三类）：
- 显性确认："在实施显性确认时，系统会暂停进一步的操作，直到收到用户的明确指示。这种确认方式特别适用于错误成本较高或需要用户明确同意的场景，例如在执行不可逆操作前的用户确认。"；"AI 必须在采取最终行动之前，明确地向用户展示操作的具体细节，并主动请求用户的明确同意。"
- 隐性确认："通过在后续的对话或操作中隐含地确认用户的信息，而无需用户进行直接回复。这种策略通常涉及系统对用户输入的关键信息进行重复或同义词替换"；"适用于错误成本较低或系统对识别结果有较高信心的情况"；劣势："一旦系统识别错误，用户可能不清楚如何纠正"。
- 无需确认："在用户输入明确且系统能够以高置信度识别用户意图的情况下，可以省略确认步骤。"；"在错误成本较高或需要用户明确同意的场景中，如执行不可逆操作前，显性确认或隐性确认可能更为合适"。

错误：三类错误（AI 无法解析用户输入／用户提供了无效输入／AI 当前功能无法满足）；机制四条："及时识别并明确指出问题所在；提供清晰的指导，帮助用户纠正错误或提供有效的替代方案；保持友好和专业的语气，以减少用户的挫败感；记录错误情况，以便不断改进 AI 的理解和响应能力。"；承认错误："AI 应在两次尝试理解用户意图失败后，主动结束对话。"；系统错误处理五条："明确错误信息；解释原因；提供解决方案；引导用户；记录和分析"。

结束："除非会丢失重大进展，否则不要进行二次确认。"（用户放弃时允许直接退出。）

### 5.6 Hybrid UI 混合界面（原文要点）
- 用户行为变化："由原来人主要的执行行为（DO），可以增加人为 AI 来提供意图信息（Chat），让 AI 去执行任务，人可以感知 AI 做的过程和结果，并做辅助决策。"
- 三类界面模式："**Do 为主：** 以界面操作为主，偶尔唤起 AI 快捷指令。例如 Quick Bar、固定指令式、内嵌生成式等。"；"**Do + Chat 均衡：** 自然语言与界面操作均衡配合。例如双区联动交互、交互式操作气泡等。"；"**Chat 为主：** 以自然语言为主，几乎没有界面操作。例如侧边式 Copilot、独立 Web Bot 等。"
- 用户感知 AI 的四个阶段（资产按此组织）："1. 唤醒阶段：用户要了解 AI 产品的能力与用法。…告知用户 AI 可实现的意图范围和预期。"；"2. 表达阶段：用户向 AI 准确表达意图。"；"3. 确认阶段：用户等待 AI 完成任务。设计目标是让用户知道该任务的AI执行运转情况。"；"4. 反馈阶段：用户核查 AI 完成结果并应用。设计目标是让用户清晰看到并信任AI任务完成的情况、并快速应用AI生成结果。"
- AI 标识："人工智能需要一些具有代表性的视觉符号，以便与人类头像或其他图标区分开来。"；"四芒星闪光点极为常见，通常代表着有 AI 的功能，也用于区分由 AI 生成或借助 AI 生成的信息。"
- 用户输入：文本、语音（"可以提供用户以语音的形式输入，转化为文字供用户确认"；异常"需要及时且清楚告知用户，如超时提醒、因网络异常而中断"）、文件、快捷命令、槽位填词、引用输入；"停止生成操作常见位于输入框附近"。
- 生成过程："处理过程反馈需要通过流式输出等方式给予用户及时响应，让用户清晰感知 AI 的工作状态，建立恰当的等待预期。在部分业务场景中，需要完整展现 AI 的思考过程和处理阶段，减少黑盒感，提升用户对系统的信任度和理解度。"；流式输出"默认使用打字机光标效果…但在模型响应速度非常快时，应直接展示完整内容"；生成失败"及时展示失败原因和可采取的措施"；生成终止"终止后保留已生成内容，清晰提示当前状态"；思考细节"通过可展开/收起的形式呈现 AI 推理的具体步骤和依据"。
- 结果展示：内容折叠；"内容参考来源展示——在 RAG 等需要提供引用依据的场景下使用。参考来源默认收起以减少干扰…来源信息需简洁清晰，增强内容可信度。"
- 结果应用：复制、重新生成（"支持多个版本结果的切换对比"）、反馈（"通过点赞/点踩收集用户对内容的评价…可通过弹窗收集具体反馈，提供快捷选项的同时保留自由输入的方式"）、删除（"考虑到操作的不可逆性，使用警示色提醒，并设置二次确认机制"）、朗读、引用。

### 5.7 Ant Design X 最独特的一两点
1. 用「意图清晰度 × 咨询/执行」决定 Do／Chat／Do+Chat 的介入方式，并给出「唤醒—表达—确认—反馈」四阶段的组件资产地图，是唯一一家把「什么时候不要用对话」讲清楚的体系。
2. 把「确认」拆成显性／隐性／无需三档并绑定错误成本与置信度；把「两次失败即承认局限并结束」写成硬规则。

### 5.8 对「AI 出草稿 → 人确认 → 归档」最有用的三条
- 显性／隐性／无需确认三档：归档、写正式表、外发任务＝显性确认；字段回填＝隐性确认（在草稿里复述识别到的值）；纯只读查询＝无需确认。
- 「Do 为主」的内嵌式与 Quick Bar：销售主线是拜访确认表单，AI 以内嵌生成、快捷指令介入，而不是让销售去聊天页。
- 生成过程与结果应用：语音转结构化用流式＋阶段提示；结果卡片标配「重新生成（多版本对比）／点赞点踩＋原因／引用来源」。

### 6.7 Anthropic：Claude Developer Platform 与 Claude Agent SDK（原文）；「Building effective agents」（二手）
- 可达文档：
  - Tool use overview：https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/overview ："Claude determines when to call a tool based on the user's request and the tool's description.   - Define tools：https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/define-tools ，"Best practices for tool definitions"："**Provide extremely detailed descriptions.** … What the tool does / When it should be used (and when it shouldn't) / What each parameter means and how it affects the tool's behavior / Any important caveats or limitations"；"**Consolidate related operations into fewer tools.**"；"**Use meaningful namespacing in tool names.**"；"**Design tool responses to return only high-signal information.** Return semantic, stable identifiers (for example, slugs or UUIDs) rather than opaque internal references"。并指向博文「Writing tools for agents」（anthropic.com 被拦截，未读）。
  - Agent SDK 权限：https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/permissions 。权限评估六步原文顺序："Hooks → Deny rules → Ask rules → Permission mode → Allow rules → canUseTool callback"；模式表：default（"calls that need approval and match no allow rule trigger your canUseTool callback"）、dontAsk（"Deny instead of prompting"）、acceptEdits、bypassPermissions（"Use with caution"）、plan（"Claude explores and plans without editing your source files; file edits are never auto-approved and prompt through your canUseTool callback"）、auto（"A model classifier approves or denies permission prompts"）。"This lets you start restrictive and loosen permissions as trust builds"。
  - Agent SDK 审批与澄清：https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-sdk/user-input ："Claude requests user input in two situations: when it needs permission to use a tool (like deleting files or running commands), and when it has clarifying questions (via the AskUserQuestion tool). Claude sees this message and may adjust its approach."；澄清问题格式："Array of 2-4 choices, each with label and description"，可加"Other"自由输入。
- 「Building effective agents」（https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents ，被拦截）：官方 cookbook（https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/claude-cookbooks/main/patterns/agents/README.md ）证实其工作流分类："Prompt Chaining / Routing / Multi-LLM Parallelization / Orchestrator-Subagents / Evaluator-Optimizer"。搜索摘要中的要点（措辞未核对）："Agents can pause for human feedback at checkpoints or when encountering blockers"；主张保持实现简单、给模型「地面真值」（ground truth）与停止条件。
- 最独特之处：把「人在回路」实现为分层权限（规则→模式→回调），且区分「审批」与「澄清提问」两种打断；拒绝时要给理由让模型调整；工具描述要写清「何时不该用」。
- 对本项目最有用的三条：
  1. Read／Draft 类工具走 allow 规则自动放行，Command／External 类走 ask 规则进入人工回调；「plan 模式」对应「只出方案不写表」。
  2. 授权卡要显示 tool_name 与完整 input；拒绝必须填理由并回传（与本项目「拒绝必须填意见并推送发起人」一致）。
  3. 澄清问题用 2～4 个选项＋「其他」，对应语音口述不清时的候选字段值。

## 9. AI 时代原则清单（候选，12 条）

以下每条给出：支持方（仅列已核实原文者，二手来源加「（二手）」）、各家用词差异、在「销售智助」里的具体表现示例。示例引用的产品口径来自本仓库 CLAUDE.md（16 项拜访基础字段、首次拜访加 4 项、「下一步」须含时间＋目标、质量评分门槛 70 分、赢单概率五档、四象限、Tool 四类、RLS 权限、任务拒绝须填意见并推送发起人、即时总结／周报导出）。

### P1 AI 只出草稿，人拍板（Draft, don't decide）
- 支持：Apple "Keep people in control"、"ask for confirmation before performing a significant action on someone's behalf"；Microsoft G9；Ant X 显性确认（"AI 必须在采取最终行动之前，明确地向用户展示操作的具体细节，并主动请求用户的明确同意"）；Carbon Revert；OpenAI needs_approval；Anthropic canUseTool／plan；AI Elements Confirmation；Microsoft 课程 HITL；Salesforce "Mindful Friction"（二手）。
- 用词差异：Apple 讲「agency／in control」；Ant X 讲「确认」并按错误成本分档；Salesforce 叫「有意识的摩擦」；工程侧叫「approval／interruption／permission」。
- 销售智助表现：语音口述→结构化草稿卡；16 项字段逐项「确认／改／补」，全部有值才允许「归档」；象限、风险、画像由 Agent 依已确认事实重算，界面不提供拖动或改分入口，只提供「对依据提异议」。

### P2 明示 AI 在场，并说清能做什么、做得多好（Disclose & set expectations）
- 支持：Carbon 四总则（AI label、每层级透明、不作装饰）；Apple "Communicate where your app uses AI"、"Never trick someone…"、"Set clear expectations…"；Microsoft G1、G2；Ant X AI 标识、"提供意图预期"、开始语"明确设定预期"；Atlassian Rovo 图标（二手）；中国《标识办法》显式／隐式标识（二手）。
- 用词差异：IBM 讲 AI presence／transparency；Apple 讲 disclosure＋expectations；微软拆成「能做什么」与「做得多好」两条；Ant X 讲「意图预期」。
- 销售智助表现：AI 抽取的字段带 AI 标记，销售改过即去标记并保留「恢复 AI 建议」；首次使用与新能力上线时用 2～3 句说明「能听懂什么、常见听不清的情况」；导出的即时总结／周报在页脚带「由 AI 生成、经 XX 确认」字样，元数据写入生成信息。

### P3 给依据与把握程度，不裸露数字（Evidence & calibrated confidence）
- 支持：Microsoft G11（并警告过度信任）；Apple Attribution／Confidence（"Because you…"；语义分档；低置信先确认或不展示）；Carbon 解释弹层四段模板；Ant X "必须写明引用的数据源"、参考来源默认收起；AI Elements Sources／Inline Citation；PAIR worksheet "Thresholds Of Uncertainty"。
- 用词差异：Apple 用 attribution（归因）区别于 explanation；IBM 用 explainability 分层（摘要→细节）；微软区分 local／global explanations。
- 销售智助表现：象限卡片一句依据（「高潜力：预算 ≥ … ；浅关系：近 30 天无高层动作」），点开看证据链到原始拜访记录；置信用「高／中／低」＋触发条件而非百分比；「低」时字段留空并给候选。

### P4 可撤销、可追溯（Undo & audit）
- 支持：Microsoft G9-C "Undo automated actions"、G16；Apple "revert or retry"、修正"persist"；Carbon Revert；AI Elements Checkpoint；OpenAI RunState 持久化＋版本标记；Microsoft 课程 "audit logging"；Salesforce audit trail 字段（二手）；项目原则「关键动作可确认可追溯」。
- 用词差异：设计侧说 undo／revert／checkpoint；工程侧说 audit trail／run state／versioning。
- 销售智助表现：归档后保留草稿版本与「谁在何时确认了什么」；每条 Agent 建议记录提示模板版本、输入事实快照、输出、人工处置（采纳／修改／拒绝＋理由）；任务拒绝意见随记录推送发起人。

### P5 行动前预览与授权，按工具类别分级（Preview & authorize before acting）
- 支持：Apple "Consider consequences and get permission before performing irreversible…"；Ant X 显性确认；OpenAI needs_approval（fail closed）、readOnlyHint 注解；Anthropic 权限六步、plan 模式、"Approve / Approve with changes / Approve and remember / Reject / Suggest alternative"；Microsoft 课程 "Pre-action approval gates, risk tiering"、"need-only basis"。
- 用词差异：Apple 讲 irreversible／significant action；OpenAI 讲 sensitive tool calls；Anthropic 讲 permission mode／ask rules；微软讲 risk tiering。
- 销售智助表现：Read／Draft 类自动执行且只读；Command 类（写客户表、下发任务、改商机阶段）与 External 类（发消息、调外部系统）弹出授权卡——显示动作、对象、参数与影响范围，提供「本次允许／总是允许（本类）／拒绝并说明」；无法解析参数时一律转人工。

### P6 失败优雅降级，不确定就问、就收缩（Graceful failure & scope when in doubt）
- 支持：Microsoft G10；Apple Limitations（预设期望、示范、解释差结果）、非 AI 回退、guided corrections（语音转写给候选）；Ant X 错误处理四条、"两次尝试…失败后，主动结束对话"、追问与消歧；Anthropic AskUserQuestion（2～4 选项＋其他）；PAIR「Errors + Graceful Failure」（二手章节名）。
- 用词差异：微软叫 scope services／gracefully degrade；Apple 叫 limitations／fallback；Ant X 叫 追问／承认错误／兜底。
- 销售智助表现：听不清的字段给 2～4 个候选；两次仍不确定则留空交人填；ASR 或模型不可用时整页退回 16 项手工表单，已识别部分保留。

### P7 不打断主线，顺势介入（Right timing, easy to ignore）
- 支持：Microsoft G3、G8；Apple 反馈入口"doesn't interrupt the experience"；Carbon "show explanations only when needed or requested"；Ant X "Do 为主" 内嵌式、Quick Bar、跟随场景出现入口、"不要让用户发出固定指令"；OpenAI Apps SDK "Helpful UI only"（二手）；Microsoft Learn "Conversation-first／Progressive complexity"（二手）。
- 用词差异：微软讲 timing／dismissal；Ant X 讲 Do／Chat 混合与介入方式；OpenAI 讲 UI 只在澄清、输入、结构化结果时出现。
- 销售智助表现：主线是拜访确认表单与客户页，AI 以内嵌补全、角标提醒、卡片建议出现；跟进异常（一周无跟进）在客户卡片角标提示，可一键忽略并记录原因；不做独立聊天首页。

### P8 渐进披露，按需展开（Progressive disclosure）
- 支持：Carbon "Start with summarized explanations"、Focused／Broad 两级；Ant X 内容折叠、思考细节可展开、来源默认收起；AI Elements Reasoning／Tool 可折叠；Apple attributions "Avoid being too specific or too general"。
- 用词差异：IBM 讲 first layer of explainability；Ant X 讲 折叠／展开；Vercel 用 collapsible 组件表达。
- 销售智助表现：草稿卡默认只显示字段值与 AI 标记；解释、原文片段、替代候选折叠在字段后；周报默认摘要，明细可展开。

### P9 过程可见、流式反馈、状态具体（Show progress specifically）
- 支持：Apple "Factor processing time"、"specific, reassuring feedback during generation"；Ant X 生成过程（流式、进度、终止保留已生成内容、失败给原因与措施）；AI Elements Reasoning／Plan／Task／Loader；Semi 消息状态；TDesign 流式与思考过程（二手）。
- 用词差异：Apple 反对"Processing…"这类泛化文案；Ant X 讲「减少黑盒感」。
- 销售智助表现：「正在转写（0:42/1:10）」→「正在提取 16 项基础字段 12/16」→「正在核对『下一步』是否含时间与目标」；中途取消保留已提取字段。

### P10 隐私最小化，数据边界清楚（Privacy & least privilege）
- 支持：Apple Privacy 三条（本地优先、最小共享、先许可、可退出、说明是否用于训练）；Microsoft "Privacy and security"；Microsoft 课程 "need-only basis"；Ant X 敏感话题回避、审慎处理未请求内容；项目原则「权限由服务端判定、RLS 兜底」。
- 用词差异：Apple 从终端用户视角讲 permission／opt out；工程侧讲 least privilege／access control。
- 销售智助表现：Agent 只能读当前销售（或主管的直属团队）有权看的客户与记录；语音原文保留期与用途在确认页可见；导出周报默认脱敏联系人电话；董事长视图只读核心客户且无原始录音。

### P11 不伪装成人，角色一致、语气克制（Don't impersonate; consistent persona）
- 支持：Apple "Never trick someone into thinking they're interacting with … a human"；Microsoft G5、G6；Ant X 角色一致性（性格、视觉、语言、行为四个一致）、会话原则「自然友好并且尊重用户」；Atlassian Rovo "honest about what it knows, clear about its limits"（二手）；Microsoft Learn "differentiating from humans"（二手）。
- 用词差异：Apple／微软从「不欺骗、不偏见」立论；Ant X 从「角色设计」立论（一致、自然、有人情味）。
- 销售智助表现：助手有固定名字与 AI 标识，不用真人头像；对客文案由 AI 起草时明示「草稿」，只有销售确认后才以销售名义发出；语气专业克制，用中文全角标点，不用夸饰。

### P12 持续校准，谨慎更新，反馈有回响（Feedback loops & cautious adaptation）
- 支持：Microsoft G13–G18（学习、谨慎更新、细粒度反馈、传达后果、全局控制、通知变更）；Apple explicit／implicit feedback（自愿、直白措辞、"Act immediately… and persist"）、"Let people share feedback on outputs"；Ant X 点赞点踩＋原因、重新生成多版本对比；Anthropic "Approve and remember"；PAIR「Feedback + Control」（二手）。
- 用词差异：微软强调「传达后果」与「通知变更」；Apple 强调「即时生效并持久化」。
- 销售智助表现：销售对 AI 抽取值的每次修改作为质量信号回流；点踩必须选原因（听错／理解错／格式错／不该生成）；质量评分规则（70 分门槛）或象限算法调整时，在首页公告并附「对我的客户有何影响」。

### 候选原则与四条不可破坏原则的对应
- 同一对象、同一事实 → P3（依据链指向同一条拜访事实）、P4（版本与追溯）。
- 统一权限 → P5、P10。
- 关键动作可确认可追溯 → P1、P4、P5。
- Agent 只生成草稿和洞察，写正式业务表必须经人工确认 → P1、P2、P5。
